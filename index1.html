About the club

Radio Raaga is the creative audio hub of our college, giving students a platform to host shows, learn production, and share stories with the entire campus community.

What we do

Host music hours, talk shows, interviews, live event coverage, and festival specials that capture the pulse of campus life.

Why it matters

Members gain real‑world experience in scripting, anchoring, sound design, and content management while building confidence.

Who can join

Any student with interest in music, audio, media, or event management, regardless of previous experience, is welcome.
